FLOOD RESPONSE STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (adapted from NDMA guidelines)

1. ZONE CLASSIFICATION: Zones with flood extent above 30% of surveyed area are
classified as high-priority for boat-based evacuation. Zones between 10-30%
require monitoring with ground vehicle standby.

2. RESOURCE DISPATCH ORDER: Rescue teams are dispatched first to zones marked
"destroyed" or "major-damage" with confirmed population presence. Boats are
reserved for zones exceeding the 30% flood-extent threshold. Ambulances are
pre-positioned at zones with population estimates above 500 residents.

3. COMMUNICATION PROTOCOL: Public alerts must state the zone identifier,
severity classification, and expected responder ETA. Alerts must avoid
speculative casualty figures until confirmed by ground teams.

4. ESCALATION: If more than 3 zones simultaneously reach "destroyed"
classification, escalate to regional disaster command and request mutual
aid from neighboring districts.

5. SHELTER ACTIVATION: Shelters are activated when displaced population
estimates exceed the shelter's rated capacity by less than 20%. Beyond that
threshold, secondary shelter sites must be identified within 2km.
